About Me

Who I Am

I’m Gaspare Tocci, a London-based Web Developer, Front-End Developer, and CMS Website Administrator with a strong interest in responsive web development, digital design, and practical problem-solving.

  • Professional direction: Building practical experience in web development, front-end development, CMS, and website administration through real-world projects.
  • Personal brand: “I Want Project: Rebrand Yourself” represents my commitment to growth, self-improvement, and long-term professional development.
  • Mindset: Disciplined, curious, and motivated to learn through practical projects, experimentation, and real-world challenges.

Analytical thinking, creativity, and technical curiosity guide the way I approach web development, digital design, and practical technology projects.

What I Do

I work across Web Development, Front-End Design, CMS, and Website Administration, combining technical skills with practical problem-solving and visual design.

  • Web Development: Building responsive websites with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, semantic HTML, CSS Grid, Flexbox, and modern responsive techniques.
  • CMS & Website Administration: Working with CMS platforms, dynamic pages, website content, layouts, updates, and ongoing website maintenance.
  • Digital Design: Creating visual assets, graphics, photo editing, video content, and supporting digital media for web projects.

I enjoy combining technology, creativity, and problem-solving to build digital experiences that are clear, accessible, responsive, and visually engaging.

What I’m Looking For

I am currently looking for a fully remote entry-level opportunity in Web Development, Front-End Development, CMS, or Website Administration, where I can contribute to real projects while continuing to develop my technical skills.

  • Web development: Contribute to responsive websites, front-end interfaces, CMS-based projects, and website improvements.
  • Hands-on experience: Work on real-world projects involving development, content management, website maintenance, and problem-solving.
  • Professional growth: Learn from experienced developers and digital professionals while contributing to a collaborative team.

I’m particularly interested in teams that value continuous learning, practical problem-solving, creativity, and professional development through real projects.
