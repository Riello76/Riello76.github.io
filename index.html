About Me

Who I Am

I’m Gaspare Tocci, a London-based aspiring Web Developer, Data Analyst, and Creative Editor with a strong interest in technology, digital creativity, and continuous learning.

  • Career transition: Building a new professional path in data analytics, web development, and digital content creation.
  • Personal brand: “I Want Project: Rebrand Yourself” represents my commitment to growth, self-improvement, and long-term career reinvention.
  • Mindset: Disciplined, curious, and motivated to learn through practical projects, experimentation, and real-world challenges.

Analytical thinking, creativity, and technical curiosity are the qualities that guide both my personal projects and my long-term career transition into the technology industry.

What I Do

I have developed practical skills across Web Design & Development, Data Analytics, and Digital Content Creation, combining technical thinking with creativity and visual communication.

  • Web Development: Building responsive websites with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, semantic structure, and modern layout techniques.
  • Data Analytics: Working with Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and Tableau to clean, analyze, and visualize data for reporting and decision-making.
  • Creative Media: Producing visual content through branding, photo editing, video editing, storytelling, and social media design projects.

I enjoy combining creativity, technology, and problem-solving to design digital experiences that are clear, accessible, and visually engaging.

What I’m Looking For

I am currently seeking a full remote entry-level opportunity where I can contribute, continue learning, and grow within a supportive technology-focused environment.

  • Professional growth: Learn from experienced developers, analysts, designers, and digital professionals.
  • Hands-on experience: Contribute to real projects involving web development, data analysis, digital content, or business reporting.
  • Collaborative culture: Work in a team that values communication, continuous improvement, innovation, and knowledge sharing.

I’m particularly interested in teams that encourage continuous learning, practical problem-solving, creativity, and professional development through real projects.
